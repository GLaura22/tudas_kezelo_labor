       Responsibilities of senior management
       According to Article 20, the top management of essential and important entities:

       - must oversee their implementation, and
       - can be held liable if cybersecurity is not implemented properly.

       Risk-based approach to cybersecurity
       Article 21 requires cybersecurity measures to be appropriate for the related risks.
       When assessing risks, entities must take into account:
       
       - exposure to risks,
       - company size,
       - likelihood and severity of incidents,
       - societal and economic impacts.

Recommended next steps:

- Obtain senior management support
Without active senior management commitment, NIS2 implementation will be slow, underfunded, and ineffective.

- Periodic management review
Management reviews ensure informed cybersecurity decisions.

- Execute corrective actions
Corrective actions prevent recurrence of nonconformities.

- Start with a gap analysis
To start, you should check what kind of cybersecurity you already have to avoid duplication of effort later on.

- Define the Risk Management Methodology
Risk management is the foundation of NIS 2 compliance.

- Perform risk assessment and treatment
Identify assets, threats, vulnerabilities, and apply cybersecurity measures accordingly.

- Write and approve the Risk Treatment Plan
This plan defines how cybersecurity measures will be implemented.

- Set up incident reporting
Establish reporting procedures for significant incidents.

- Set up the assessment of cybersecurity effectiveness
Use monitoring, audits, and management review.

- Periodic internal audit
Internal audits help identify nonconformities.
