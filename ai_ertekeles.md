# NIS2 Compliance Audit Report – Executive Summary
**Company:** sinoeirnoern
**Report Date:** 2026-05-06

---

## **Introduction**
The **NIS2 Directive (Directive (EU) 2022/2555)** establishes a comprehensive framework for cybersecurity risk management and incident reporting across the European Union. As an entity falling under its scope, **sinoeirnoern** is required to implement robust cybersecurity measures to protect critical infrastructure and ensure operational resilience.

For further details, consult the official documents:
- [NIS2 Directive (EU 2022/2555)](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dir/2022/2555)
- [NIS2 Implementation Guide](https://advisera.com/articles/nis2-implementation-steps/)

This report outlines the identified gaps in compliance and provides actionable recommendations to achieve full alignment with NIS2 requirements.

---

## **Key Compliance Gaps**

### **1. Senior Management Accountability (Article 20)**
NIS2 mandates that **senior management** must take an active role in cybersecurity governance. Currently, the company lacks documented evidence that leadership has:
- **Approved cybersecurity measures** to mitigate identified risks.
- **Overseen their implementation**, as required by Articles 20, 32, and 33.
- **Established liability frameworks** for non-compliance, which could expose legal representatives to administrative fines.

**Missing Documentation:**
- **Internal Audit Report** (to validate oversight).
- **Risk Treatment Plan** (to formalize approved measures).

##

### **2. Risk-Based Cybersecurity Approach (Article 21)**
A **risk-driven strategy** is essential under NIS2, yet the company has not fully assessed:
- **Exposure risks** (e.g., vulnerabilities in critical systems).
- **Societal and economic impacts** of potential cyber incidents, which must be factored into risk evaluations.

**Missing Documentation:**
- **Risk Assessment Methodology** (to define evaluation criteria).
- **Risk Treatment Plan** (to document mitigation strategies).

##

### **3. Incident Analysis & Reporting (Article 23)**
NIS2 requires structured **incident handling**, including:
- **Categorization of incidents** by severity and impact.
- **Timely reporting** of significant incidents to competent authorities.

**Missing Documentation:**
- **Incident Handling Policy** (to standardize response procedures).
- **Significant Incident Notification** (to comply with reporting obligations).

##

### **4. Supervision of Cybersecurity Measures (Article 21 & 34)**
Effective oversight mechanisms are lacking, particularly:
- **On-site inspections** to verify compliance.
- **Security scans** to detect vulnerabilities.
- **Internal audits** to assess cybersecurity effectiveness.

**Missing Documentation:**
- **Internal Audit Report** (to track compliance status).

Non-compliance with these requirements may result in **administrative fines** under Article 34.

---

## **Recommended Actions**

To achieve NIS2 compliance, sinoeirnoern should prioritize the following steps:

1. **Secure Senior Management Commitment**
   - Leadership must formally approve cybersecurity measures and allocate necessary resources.
   - Establish accountability frameworks to ensure oversight and liability.

2. **Conduct a Gap Analysis**
   - Assess current cybersecurity practices to identify redundancies and gaps.

3. **Develop a Risk Management Framework**
   - Define a **Risk Assessment Methodology** to evaluate threats, vulnerabilities, and impacts.
   - Perform a **risk assessment** and draft a **Risk Treatment Plan** to address findings.

4. **Implement Incident Reporting Procedures**
   - Create an **Incident Handling Policy** and establish channels for reporting significant incidents.

5. **Strengthen Oversight Mechanisms**
   - Schedule **periodic internal audits** and **security scans** to monitor compliance.
   - Conduct **management reviews** to ensure informed decision-making.

6. **Execute Corrective Actions**
   - Address identified nonconformities to prevent recurrence.

---

## **Conclusion**
While sinoeirnoern has taken steps toward cybersecurity, **critical gaps remain in governance, risk management, and incident reporting**. Addressing these deficiencies through structured implementation and leadership engagement will ensure compliance with NIS2 and enhance resilience against cyber threats.
